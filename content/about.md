# About One Degree

One Degree is a nonprofit that helps low-income individuals and families find free and low-cost services near them. Its main product is 1degree.org, a free directory of more than 30,000 nonprofit and government resources.

## What you can find
Each listing describes what a service offers (food, shelter, health care, job training, legal aid, benefits enrollment), who is eligible, where it is, when it is open, which languages it supports, how accessible it is and how to contact it.

## How it works
Tell us what you need in plain words. If you create a free profile, we match you with providers near you and can send a request on your behalf. You decide what to share. Providers only see your details after you agree.

## Cost
Using One Degree is free for people looking for help.

## For providers
Organizations can log in to keep their listing current, see requests from people who need help and upload policy documents. Changes to a listing are reviewed by One Degree staff before they go live.

## Keeping listings accurate
Listings go out of date, so One Degree checks them against each organization's website on a regular schedule and its staff review anything that looks wrong. The last verified date is shown on each listing.
